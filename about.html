About Us

Tech Lo Tech Hub (kangleipungweb.in) is a free, open library of papers and technical knowledge. Our aim is simple: researchers and developers share what they know, and everyone can read it for free, with no account and no paywall.

Who runs this site

Tech Lo Tech Hub (kangleipungweb.in) is operated under Kangleipung Initiative and run by an independent editorial team. We do not write the papers ourselves; every paper is contributed by its author, and the author is credited by name on the paper’s page.

How we review papers

  1. Submission: authors send their paper through our submission page with their name, a summary, and a PDF.
  2. Originality check: we only publish original work. We check submissions for copied or previously published material, and we reject anything that appears plagiarized or scraped.
  3. Quality and relevance: we read each paper to check that it is clear, complete and useful to readers, and that it fits one of our categories.
  4. Rights: authors confirm they have the right to share the work and allow us to publish it on this site. See our Terms & Disclaimer.
  5. Publication with credit: approved papers are published in both readable text and downloadable PDF, credited to the author.

Corrections and takedowns

If you find an error, or you believe a paper infringes your rights, please contact us. We take these reports seriously and respond promptly.
