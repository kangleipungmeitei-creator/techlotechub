Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

At Tech Lo Tech Hub (this website, operated under Kangleipung Initiative), protecting the privacy of our readers and contributing researchers is a core priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and recorded when you use the site and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Log files: Like most websites, this site and its hosting providers use standard log files. These may include IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, and referring and exit pages. This data is not linked to personally identifiable information and is used to analyze trends and administer the site.
  • Paper submissions and contact information: When authors submit papers or contact us directly, we collect the name, email address, affiliation and manuscript content they voluntarily provide. The author’s name and affiliation are shown as credit if the paper is published. Email addresses are never shown publicly and are used only to contact the author about the submission.
  • Author page details: If you give a short bio and links when submitting, we may show them publicly on your author page if your paper is published.
  • Issue reports: If you report a problem with a paper, we store your message and, if you give it, your email address so we can follow up. Emails are never shown publicly.

2. Cookies and web beacons

This site uses cookies and similar technologies, including those set by our advertising partners described below. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, although parts of the site may then work differently.

3. Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners

We may use Google AdSense to show advertisements on this website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites across the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can read how Google handles data in its advertising products at Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

4. Service providers

The site is served through GitHub Pages, and submissions and papers are stored with Supabase, our database and file-storage provider. These providers process data on our behalf to run the site.

5. Third-party privacy policies

This Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or to external websites linked from papers. Please read the privacy policies of those third parties for details about their practices and opt-out options.

6. Data security and your rights

We use reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS encryption and input sanitization, to protect submitted manuscripts and communications. Depending on where you live (including under GDPR and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information you have given us. Write to us at the address below.

7. Children’s information

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us such information, contact us and we will remove it promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed.

9. Consent and contact
